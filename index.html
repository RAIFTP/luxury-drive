04 — The Standard

Not every car makes the cut.

Our bar is simple: if we wouldn't hand a car to our own family, we won't hand it to yours. Four phases stand between a stranger's car and a Luxury Drive listing.

200+ checkpointsOn every listed vehicle

We buy few, and we choose slowly. Most cars offered to us never reach the floor — repainted accident shells, unverified imports and "engine perfected" stories are politely declined at the gate. What remains is a small, rotating stock we're proud to put our name on.

Every candidate goes through a 200+ point audit — engine and drivetrain, suspension and steering, electronics and airbags, air-conditioning, tyres, and a frame-level accident scan. A car that fails one point doesn't get a discount. It gets declined.

RC and ownership chain, insurance validity, service history, hypothecation and NOC, pending challans — every line is verified before we quote a price. You buy the car and the peace of mind, together.

Full machine polish, interior sanitisation and a final quality pass — then keys in hand, papers signed, and a walk-around so you know your car as well as we do. Most handovers end with photographs; ours usually end with invitations to come back for the next one.
